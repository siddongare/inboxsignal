# Impeccable Design Skill

## Core Directives
1. **Never use AI Slop tells:**
   - Avoid generic Inter/Arial default looks.
   - Avoid gray text on colored backgrounds.
   - Avoid untinted pure gray/black backgrounds (always tint slightly with cool navy, slate, or charcoal).
   - Avoid cards-inside-cards with uniform padding.
   - Avoid rubbery/bounce easing.
2. **Typography & Typesetting:**
   - Strict hierarchical contrast. Headings should feel intentional and sculpted (`tracking-[-0.03em]`).
   - Reserve monospace fonts strictly for code snippets, raw metrics, and numerical data. Body text must always be crisp, highly legible sans-serif.
3. **Restrained Color & Depth:**
   - Primary neutral: Deep obsidian/carbon surfaces with subtle ambient luminosity.
   - Accent colors: Use 1-2 intentional, saturated accents (e.g., Luminous Emerald for health/success, Deep Sapphire for action).
   - Micro-borders: 1px subtle strokes (`border-white/[0.08]` to `border-white/[0.12]`) to give structure without visual weight.
4. **Delight & Micro-Interactions:**
   - Subtle interactive elevation and lighting shifts on hover.
   - Fluid radial score meter that conveys signal health in under 4 seconds.
